# Balance check: owner's guide

## One time
1. Unzip this folder anywhere (for example your Documents).
2. Double-click **setup.bat**. Follow the questions: pick your Excel list, pick the reply folder, type the boss's email.
3. When it says "Setup finished", use the **Balance Check** file on your Desktop (or double-click **start.bat**).

The first weeks it runs in **TRIAL mode**: your original Excel is never changed. It makes a dated copy in
`Documents\BalanceCheck\reports` for you to compare with your own. To switch to updating your real Excel, ask the person
who set it up to change one setting (`write_mode` to `in_place`). A backup is kept before every change.

## Every day
1. Open **Balance Check** (a black window opens, then the page in your browser). Do not close the black window while you work.
2. Press **Look for new replies** (or **Pull from Outlook** if it shows).
3. Press **Start checking**. For each reply look at the customer, our balance and the attached letter:
   - If the reply agrees: **Signed, same as ours**, then **Correct, save**.
   - If the customer states a different balance, check the number and press **Correct, save**.
   - If you can't read it, type the balance yourself, or press **Skip for now**.
   - If it is not a balance confirmation at all: **Not a balance confirmation**.
4. Check **Checks** (a number shows when something looks wrong) and **maybe replies** (mails the tool was not sure about).
5. **MIS report**: press **Open MIS in Excel**, then **Email MIS to boss**. A draft opens in Outlook with the file attached. You read it and press Send yourself.

## If something goes wrong
Take a photo or screenshot of the page or the black window and send it to the person who set this up.
Nothing is ever deleted, and nothing is sent without you pressing Send.
